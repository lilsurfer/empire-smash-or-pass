# England Empire: Smash or Pass 👑🔥

A feature-packed, Tinder-style "Smash or Pass" web game featuring all **51 iconic character portraits and variants** extracted directly from [england-empire.web.app](https://england-empire.web.app).

---

## 🎮 Features

- **All 51 Empire Characters & Multiverse Variants**:
  - The James Multiverse (James, Farmer James, Sleepy James, Pirate James, Maid James, etc.)
  - The Oli Continuum (Oli, Oli final form, Wise Oli, Silly Oli, Sus Oli 67, etc.)
  - The Leo Dynasty (Leo, Ginger Leo, Driving Leo, Shower Leo, Leo final form)
  - The Lucas Order (Lucas, Serious Lucas, Meta Lucas, Digging Lucas, Unsuspecting Lucas)
  - The Jake Syndicate (Jake, Awesome Sauce Jake, Goggle Jake, Fishing Jake, AHHH Jake)
  - Crown & Faculty (Papa Brogden, Gran Brogden, Mr Blunden, Cutie Mr Blunden, Mr Douglas, Steve the Seagull, Bailey the Dog)
  - Mythic Legends (Stephen, Stephen FINAL FORM, Harrison, Harrison 2.0, Erika, Erik 2, Ethan, Dale, etc.)

- **Tinder-Style Gesture & Physics Engine**:
  - Drag / swipe with mouse or touch screen.
  - Smooth spring physics, 3D card tilt, and multi-layer depth elevation.
  - Dynamic visual stamps: **SMASH** (Right), **PASS** (Left), **SUPER SMASH** (Up).

- **🦅 Live Commentary by Gerald the Seagull**:
  - Gerald watches from his wheelie bin with his stolen teabag crown.
  - Over 30 custom situational dialogue reactions based on who you choose.

- **📖 Gerald's Secret Dossier (Card Flip)**:
  - Tap any card or hit `Space` to flip to the classified intel report with lore, threat levels, and backstories.

- **🎵 Procedural Web Audio Engine**:
  - Zero-latency synthesized sound effects for Smash, Pass, Super Smash, Undo, Card Flip, and Royal Fanfare.
  - Full mute toggle.

- **✨ Royal Coronation & Endgame Screen**:
  - Evaluates your Imperial Smash Ratio and crowns you with a custom Royal Title.
  - Confetti particle explosion (pure canvas).
  - One-click "Share Verdict" to copy your royal scorecard.

- **🗂️ Empire Match Center & Clan Filters**:
  - Filter by clan or play all 51.
  - Review your Smashed, Super Smashed, and Pass Bin lists with the ability to modify decisions.

## ⌨️ Controls & Shortcuts

| Action | Mouse / Touch | Keyboard |
|---|---|---|
| **Pass** | Drag Left / Click ❌ | `←` or `A` |
| **Smash** | Drag Right / Click 🔥 | `→` or `D` |
| **Super Smash** | Drag Up / Click ⭐ | `↑` or `W` |
| **Undo** | Click ↩️ | `Z` or `Backspace` |
| **Inspect Dossier** | Tap Card / Click 📖 | `Space` or `I` |
| **Mute Audio** | Click 🔊 | `M` |

## 🚀 Running Locally

```bash
# Using Python
python3 -m http.server 3000 --directory /home/antigravity/empire-smash-or-pass

# Or using npx
npx serve /home/antigravity/empire-smash-or-pass
```
Open `http://localhost:3000` in your browser.
